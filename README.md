## What it does

Every one of these is shown, moving, at **[winnotch.xyz](https://winnotch.xyz)**.

- **Live activities.** Charging, headphones connecting, a muted mic, a finished download, the end of a focus session. Each plays inside the same 204 × 38 notch, then gets out of your way.
- **Coding agents.** Connect Claude Code or Codex once. When a run finishes or stops to ask something, the notch tells you, and a click takes you back to its terminal.
- **Music.** Spotify's artwork and a live wave beside the clock, in the album's own colours. Volume keys show a dotted level instead of the Windows pop-up.
- **Sports.** Follow your teams and their live scores sit beside the clock: football, cricket, basketball and every other sport ESPN covers. When your side scores, so does your notch.
- **Three modes, one key.** <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> slides between Time, Stats and Large sport. Fullscreen games keep a mode of their own, and the notch can stay out of them entirely.
- **Search.** <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, then just type: tasks, past notes, saved text, macros, quick maths and unit conversions.
- **Screen text.** <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>T</kbd>, then drag over anything on screen to copy its words. Recognition runs on your PC.
- **A whole workspace.** Hover, and tasks, focus timers, notes, a file shelf, live system graphs, launchers and a calculator open in place. Move away and it tucks itself back in.

It never appears in the taskbar or Alt+Tab, and your tasks, notes and settings stay on your PC.
